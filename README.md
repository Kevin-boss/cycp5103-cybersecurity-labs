## Ethics & scope
- Active scanning only against `127.0.0.1` or VMs I control.
- Public-site research is passive only (no login, enumeration or scanning).
- No real credentials, OTPs, phone numbers or third-party IPs are committed.
- Phishing work is awareness/defence only; nothing is sent to real recipients.
